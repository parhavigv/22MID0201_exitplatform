# Exit Interview Policy
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective From:** 2025-01-01
**Owner:** Human Resources

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes how exit interviews are scheduled, conducted, recorded, and used. The exit interview is a formal stage of the offboarding workflow (`EXIT_INTERVIEW`) and is a required step before full-and-final settlement is released.

---

## 2. Scope

All employees leaving the organisation by resignation, retirement, or end of contract. Employees exiting through termination for cause are handled separately by HR and are not covered by this policy.

---

## 3. When the Exit Interview Happens

The exit interview takes place after clearance stages (IT, Finance, Admin and Asset Return) are substantially complete and before final settlement is processed.

| Item | Standard |
|---|---|
| Scheduled by | Human Resources |
| Conducted by | An HR representative who is **not** the employee's reporting manager |
| Timing | Within the last 5 working days of the notice period |
| Duration | 30–45 minutes |
| Format | In person, or video call for remote and hybrid employees |
| Completion deadline | On or before the last working day |

If the employee's last working day falls on a company holiday, the interview is moved to the last preceding working day.

---

## 4. Why the Manager Does Not Conduct It

The interview is deliberately conducted by HR rather than the reporting manager. Candid feedback about management, workload, or team culture is difficult to give to the person it concerns. The manager receives themes in aggregate, never a verbatim transcript attributed to the individual.

---

## 5. What the Interview Covers

The structured questionnaire covers six areas:

1. **Reason for leaving** — the primary and any contributing factors.
2. **Role and responsibilities** — whether the job matched expectations set at hiring.
3. **Manager and team** — support, feedback quality, and working relationships.
4. **Growth and development** — learning opportunities, promotion, and career path.
5. **Compensation and benefits** — perceived fairness relative to market and to peers.
6. **Organisation and culture** — what the company should keep doing, and what it should change.

A free-text section at the end lets the employee raise anything the structured questions missed.

---

## 6. Is the Exit Interview Mandatory?

Participation is **strongly encouraged but voluntary**. An employee may decline the interview, or decline to answer any individual question, without affecting:

- Their full-and-final settlement
- Their relieving letter or experience letter
- Any future reference check

If an employee declines, HR records the task as waived with a note. The `EXIT_INTERVIEW` stage is then closed and the workflow proceeds to final settlement.

---

## 7. Confidentiality and Who Sees the Responses

| Audience | What they see |
|---|---|
| HR Business Partner | Full responses, attributed |
| Reporting manager | Aggregated themes only, never attributed verbatim |
| Department head | Aggregated themes across their department |
| Leadership | Quarterly trend reporting, fully anonymised |

Responses are never shared with the employee's manager in a form that identifies who said what. Where a team is small enough that a theme would identify an individual, the theme is held back and reported only at department level.

---

## 8. Automated Summarisation

Submitted responses are analysed by the platform's Exit Interview Summarizer, which produces a sentiment rating, a list of recurring themes, and a short narrative summary. This summary is stored against the exit case.

The analysis assists HR; it does not replace the HR representative's own reading of the responses. No action is taken against any person named in an exit interview on the basis of the automated summary alone.

---

## 9. Raising a Serious Concern

An exit interview is **not** the correct channel for reporting harassment, discrimination, fraud, or a safety violation. Those must be raised through the formal grievance or whistleblower process, which carries protections and an investigation obligation that an exit interview does not.

If such a concern surfaces during an exit interview, the HR representative will stop, explain the formal channel, and help the employee raise it there. The concern is escalated the same day regardless of whether the employee chooses to file formally.

---

## 10. Retention

Exit interview responses are retained for 24 months from the last working day, then deleted. Aggregated and anonymised theme data is retained indefinitely for trend analysis and cannot be traced back to an individual.

---

## 11. Rescheduling and No-Shows

An employee may reschedule once, to any slot on or before the last working day. If the employee does not attend and does not reschedule, HR records the task as waived and the workflow continues. A missed exit interview never blocks settlement.

---

## 12. Questions

Direct questions about the exit interview to your HR Business Partner. Questions about what happens to your feedback after submission are answered in sections 7 and 10 above.
