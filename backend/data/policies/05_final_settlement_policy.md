# Full and Final Settlement Policy

**Version:** 1.3
**Effective From:** 2025-01-01
**Owner:** Finance / Human Resources

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes what is included in the full-and-final (F&F) settlement for exiting employees, the timeline for payment, and the clearances required before payment is released.

---

## 2. Scope

All permanent employees leaving the organisation by any exit type (resignation, termination, retirement, end of contract).

---

## 3. Components of Full-and-Final Settlement

The F&F settlement includes the following components:

### 3.1 Salary Components

- **Last month's salary** — for the days actually worked in the final month (including notice period days served).
- **Notice period salary** — if the company waived part of the notice period, the waived days are paid out at the applicable per-day rate.

### 3.2 Leave Encashment

- Accrued but unused annual leave (earned leave) is encashed at the rate of basic salary ÷ 26 days × unused leave days.
- The maximum encashable balance is subject to the company's leave policy (typically capped at 30 days).
- Sick leave and casual leave that are not earned leave are not encashed.

### 3.3 Gratuity

Payable to employees who have completed **5 or more years of continuous service** with the organisation. Calculated as:

> Gratuity = (Last drawn basic salary × 15 × years of service) ÷ 26

For employees who completed less than 5 years, gratuity is not payable unless mandated by local law for the employee's work location.

### 3.4 Provident Fund (PF)

- Employee's PF contribution is fully withdrawable after leaving.
- Employer's PF contribution is withdrawable after 5 years of service. Below 5 years, the employer's share vests per applicable statutory rules.
- Finance facilitates PF transfer or withdrawal upon request. The employee must submit a PF withdrawal or transfer form during the exit process.

### 3.5 Reimbursements

Any approved, pending expense reimbursements submitted before the last working day are included in F&F. Claims submitted after the last working day will not be accepted.

### 3.6 Deductions from F&F

The following are deducted from F&F settlement before disbursement:

- Notice period shortfall (if employee left early without paying the buyout).
- Outstanding salary advances or loans taken from the company.
- Asset damage charges raised by Admin or IT.
- Personal charges on corporate credit cards.
- Any other amounts owed to the company as per HR and Finance records.

---

## 4. Required Clearances Before F&F is Released

F&F payment is only processed after ALL of the following clearances are confirmed in the exit system:

| Clearance | Issued By |
|---|---|
| IT Clearance Certificate | IT Department |
| Asset Return Clearance Certificate | Administration |
| NDA Acknowledgement Form (signed) | Employee — witnessed by HR |
| KT Completion Sign-off | Reporting Manager |
| Finance Clearance (no outstanding dues) | Finance |
| HR Clearance | HR Business Partner |

If any clearance is pending, Finance will place the F&F on hold. The employee will be notified of the blocking clearance.

---

## 5. F&F Settlement Timeline

| Milestone | Timeline |
|---|---|
| All clearances confirmed | Day 0 (baseline) |
| F&F calculation sent to employee for review | Within 5 business days of Day 0 |
| Employee disputes (if any) raised | Within 3 business days of receiving calculation |
| Dispute resolution | Within 5 business days of dispute raised |
| F&F payment processed | Within 45 days of last working day (per statutory norms) |
| Form 16 (tax certificate) issued | By 15 June of the following financial year |

---

## 6. F&F Calculation Sheet

Finance will share a detailed F&F calculation sheet with the employee showing each component and deduction itemised. The employee must sign and return the acknowledgement slip within 3 business days. Silence beyond 3 business days is treated as acceptance.

---

## 7. Relieving Letter

The relieving letter confirms the employee's last working day, tenure, and the fact that they have been relieved from all duties and obligations (except those that survive under the NDA and non-compete).

The relieving letter is issued **only after** all clearances are complete and F&F is processed. It is not issued in advance under any circumstances.

The relieving letter is issued in digital format through the HR portal and may also be sent to the employee's personal email registered with HR.

---

## 8. Experience Letter

An experience letter (confirming the period of employment and designation held) is issued at the same time as the relieving letter, or separately on request via the HR portal up to 2 years after the last working day.

---

## 9. Reference Checks

The company will confirm employment dates and the last designation held to authorised third parties conducting reference checks. Performance assessments and reasons for leaving are shared only with the employee's written consent.

---

## 10. Disputes

Disputes about F&F components should be raised in writing to the HR Business Partner. If unresolved, they are escalated to the HR Head and then to Finance Head. The company's calculation based on records is the starting point; the burden is on the employee to demonstrate a discrepancy with supporting documentation.

---

## 11. Tax Implications

- Last salary, leave encashment, and notice period salary are taxable as income.
- Gratuity is exempt up to the statutory limit under the Income Tax Act; any amount above the limit is taxable.
- PF withdrawal before 5 years of service is subject to TDS.

Employees are advised to consult a tax professional for personal tax planning related to F&F.
