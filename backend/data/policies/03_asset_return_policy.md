# Asset Return Policy

**Version:** 1.2
**Effective From:** 2025-01-01
**Owner:** Administration / Human Resources

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy covers the return of all physical company assets by exiting employees, the assessment of asset condition, and the process for recovering costs when assets are damaged or not returned.

---

## 2. Scope

All employees, contractors, and interns who have been issued company assets, regardless of their role, location, or mode of exit.

---

## 3. Asset Categories and Return Deadlines

| Asset Category | Return Deadline |
|---|---|
| Laptops, tablets, phones | Last working day |
| Access cards and security tokens | Last working day |
| Office keys and parking passes | Last working day |
| Furniture (home-office scheme) | Within 7 calendar days of last working day |
| Books and reference materials | Last working day |
| Company credit / corporate cards | 2 business days before last working day |

---

## 4. Asset Audit Process

### 4.1 Asset Register Pull

HR and Admin will run an asset register report for the employee within 5 business days of resignation confirmation. The report lists every asset issued against the employee's ID.

### 4.2 Employee Acknowledgement

The employee receives the asset list and must either confirm all items are in their possession or flag any discrepancies within 3 business days of receiving the list.

### 4.3 Return Appointment

Admin schedules a return appointment on or before the last working day. For remote employees, Admin coordinates courier pickup.

---

## 5. Condition Assessment

At the time of return, an Admin representative inspects each asset and classifies its condition:

| Condition | Description | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Good | Functional, cosmetically intact, normal wear | No charge |
| Fair | Minor cosmetic damage (light scratches, small dents) | No charge |
| Poor | Non-cosmetic damage affecting function or appearance beyond normal use | Repair cost charged |
| Unusable | Device does not power on, screen shattered, major physical damage | Replacement cost charged |
| Missing | Asset not returned | Replacement cost charged |

The employee has the right to dispute a condition classification within 24 hours of the assessment. Disputes are escalated to the Admin Head, whose decision is final.

---

## 6. Cost Recovery

### 6.1 Charge Calculation

Replacement cost is based on the current market price for an equivalent asset, not the original purchase price. Repair cost is based on a quote from an authorised service centre.

For assets more than 3 years old, depreciation is applied:
- Year 1–2: Full replacement cost
- Year 3: 75% of replacement cost
- Year 4+: 50% of replacement cost

### 6.2 Deduction from F&F

Costs are deducted from the employee's full-and-final settlement. If the F&F amount is insufficient, the employee must pay the balance within 30 days of the last working day. Unpaid balances may be pursued through legal or collections means.

---

## 7. Company Credit / Corporate Cards

Corporate cards must be returned to Finance 2 business days before the last working day. The employee must:

1. Submit all outstanding expense claims and supporting receipts by the card return date.
2. Ensure no personal charges appear on the card.
3. Confirm in writing that the card balance is zero.

Any unauthorised personal charges on a corporate card are deducted from F&F.

---

## 8. Home Office Assets (Remote / Hybrid Employees)

Employees issued furniture or equipment under the Home Office Scheme must arrange return within 7 calendar days of the last working day. Admin will provide:

- A pre-paid courier label (for items under 5 kg).
- Pickup coordination for heavier items.

Employees are responsible for adequate packaging to prevent transit damage. If transit damage occurs due to inadequate packaging, the cost is borne by the employee.

---

## 9. Asset Return Clearance Certificate

Admin issues an Asset Return Clearance Certificate once all items are received and assessed. This certificate is uploaded to the exit system and is required by Finance before releasing the F&F payment.

---

## 10. Non-Return of Assets

If an employee fails to return assets by the deadline and does not respond to Admin follow-up:

1. Admin notifies HR and the employee's manager.
2. HR sends a formal written notice with a 7-day deadline.
3. If still not returned, replacement costs are deducted from F&F. If F&F is insufficient, a legal recovery notice is issued.
4. The IT team is also notified, as missing devices are treated as potential data-security incidents.
